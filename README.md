# JobProof Pro Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 12, 2026*

## Overview

JobProof Pro is a field documentation and proof-of-work application designed for contractors, handymen, inspectors, property maintenance professionals, and service businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be stored or processed when using JobProof Pro.

---

## Information Collected

JobProof Pro may allow users to create and store the following information:

* Customer names
* Job addresses
* Work notes
* Materials used
* Progress updates
* Before and after photos
* Customer signatures
* Exported reports and documentation

All information entered into the app is provided directly by the user.

---

## Camera Access

JobProof Pro may request access to your device camera to allow users to capture:

* Before photos
* After photos
* Progress photos
* Job documentation images

Photos are used only for job documentation purposes within the app.

---

## Local Storage

JobProof Pro is designed to primarily store data locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to export or share data manually.

The app may store:

* Job records
* Customer records
* Photos
* Signatures
* Generated reports

---

## Sharing and Exporting

Users may choose to export or share reports, photos, or documentation through email, messaging apps, cloud storage providers, or other third-party applications.

Once shared outside JobProof Pro, the handling of that data is governed by the privacy policies of those third-party services.

---

## Data Selling

JobProof Pro does not sell personal information or customer data.

---

## Cloud Services

At this time, JobProof Pro does not claim to provide cloud synchronization or online account storage unless explicitly added in future updates.

If cloud-based features are added later, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

---

## Data Security

Users are responsible for securing their own devices, backups, exported files, and shared reports.

While reasonable efforts are made to support secure local storage, no method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## Children's Privacy

JobProof Pro is intended for business and productivity use and is not directed toward children.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as JobProof Pro evolves and new features are added.

Users are encouraged to review the latest version when updates are released.
